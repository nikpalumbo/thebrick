FAQ

Common questions

Transparency before you reach out — how we handle viewings, off-market access, and fees.

How do I access off-market properties?

Off-market listings are not on the public site. Request access via our confidential procedure: accept the privacy agreement, submit your buyer profile, and receive a personal access code after team approval (within 48 hours).

Do you work with international buyers?

Yes. We advise clients relocating to Ticino and buyers from across Europe and the Gulf — with support for legal coordination and settlement when needed.

How are viewings arranged?

Viewings are private and by appointment only. We prepare a shortlist based on your criteria, then coordinate access with owners or their representatives — always with discretion.

What are your fees?

Fees depend on the mandate (buyer representation, seller advisory, or off-market introduction). We discuss this transparently during the first consultation — no obligation.

Can you help me sell a prestige property?

Yes. We offer discreet seller advisory, premium marketing for public listings, and off-market placement when maximum confidentiality is required.
